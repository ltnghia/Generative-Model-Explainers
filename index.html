ADVERSARIAL · since 2014

GAN

A generator learns to fool a discriminator that is learning to catch fakes. At inference the generator draws an image in one forward pass.

minG maxD 𝔼[log D(x)] + 𝔼[log(1 − D(G(z)))]
  • GAN on 2-D data Goodfellow ’14
  • Conditional GAN · Pix2Pix Isola ’17
  • Text-to-image GAN ’16 → AttnGAN ’18

Live: two small MLPs train in your browser on 10 toy distributions. Pix2Pix and text-to-image use procedural illustrations of each model's behaviour.

DENOISING · since 2020

Diffusion

A network learns to undo gradual noising. Sampling starts from pure noise and removes a little at each of many steps, guided by the prompt.

xt = √ᾱt·x0 + √(1−ᾱt)·ε → predict ε (or velocity v)
  • DDPM, pixel space Ho ’20
  • Stable Diffusion, latent U-Net Rombach ’22
  • FLUX, rectified-flow MMDiT BFL ’24

Live: the exact optimal denoiser for a tiny training set, with the real noise schedules, samplers, guidance and latent shapes.

AUTOREGRESSIVE · since 2024

VAR

A GPT-style transformer predicts the image coarse-to-fine: one whole scale of tokens per step, from a 1×1 map up to the full grid.

p(r1,…,rK) = ∏k p(rk | r1,…,rk−1)
  • VAR, next-scale prediction Tian ’24
  • SWITTI, non-causal T2I Voronov ’25
  • Infinity, bitwise tokens Han ’25

Live: a working multi-scale residual quantizer encodes every picture, so each token map shown is computed, not drawn.

How many network passes does one image take?

The clearest practical difference between the families is how many times the big network must run, one after another, to produce a single image. Passes differ in cost, so read this as a count of sequential steps, not a speed benchmark.

1101001,00010,000

Log scale. Classifier-free guidance usually doubles each diffusion step (one conditional and one unconditional pass); FLUX.1 [dev] folds guidance into a single pass. Figures are the defaults described in the three explainers.

Timeline of the models covered

Colour marks the family. Every milestone listed here is explained in one of the three pages.

  1. 2014GAN (Goodfellow et al.) and conditional GAN (Mirza & Osindero)
  2. 2016GAN-INT-CLS draws birds and flowers from captions at 64×64
  3. 2017Pix2Pix: U-Net + PatchGAN for image-to-image translation; StackGAN generates in stages
  4. 2018AttnGAN adds word-level attention
  5. 2019StyleGAN's style-based generator
  6. 2020DDPM: denoising diffusion probabilistic models
  7. 2021DDIM: deterministic sampling in about 50 steps
  8. 2022Latent diffusion / Stable Diffusion; classifier-free guidance
  9. 2023GigaGAN scales a GAN to about one billion parameters
  10. 2024SD3 (rectified flow, MMDiT) and FLUX.1 [dev] / [schnell]
  11. 2024VAR: next-scale prediction, NeurIPS 2024 Best Paper
  12. 2025SWITTI and Infinity (CVPR 2025); InfinityStar for video
  13. 2025FLUX.2: 32B transformer with a vision-language text encoder
